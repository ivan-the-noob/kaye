How we would work

From first call to a running weekly rhythm

What onboarding looks like when you bring me onto your team — use the arrows, drag, or your keyboard to step through.

01

Discovery call

A short call to map what is actually eating your week — the listings waiting on remarks, the offers you are drafting at midnight, the posts that never go out. We agree on which of those I take first.

  • Review of your current transaction and listing volume
  • Which systems you already run: Matrix, KW Command, TransactionDesk
  • A short written summary of scope and priorities
02

Access & secure setup

You grant only the access the work needs. I document every credential location, set up shared folders, and confirm how signed documents and compliance files should be stored before touching a live file.

  • Logins for MLS, TransactionDesk, DocuSign and CRM
  • Google Workspace folder structure agreed and created
  • Naming conventions for pending and closed files
03

First week — shadow and template

I work alongside you on real files, checking in before anything goes out. By the end of the week your recurring documents and post formats exist as templates instead of living in your head.

  • Offer and schedule templates built in WebForms
  • Listing description format agreed for your market
  • A content calendar drafted two weeks ahead
04

The weekly rhythm

Predictable cadence so you always know what is handled. Listings prepared as they come in, offers turned around same day, content scheduled ahead, and a short status note so nothing sits silently.

  • Daily: inbox, CRM updates, scheduled content
  • Per file: paperwork drafted, conditions and dates tracked
  • Weekly: pipeline status and outstanding items
05

Review and expand

After the first month we look at what is working. If transaction coordination is running clean, we add marketing or database projects — scope grows only where it is actually earning its hours.

  • Month-one review of turnaround times and volume
  • Scope adjusted up or down, in writing
  • New workflows documented as they are added

Questions

Before you reach out

The things agents usually want to know on the first call.

Can you work my hours?
Yes. I am based in Cavite, Philippines and work full-time on shifts set around your market — US Eastern and Pacific, UK, or Australian Eastern. The live clocks above show the current overlap.
How do you handle logins and confidential documents?
Access is granted by you, limited to what the work actually needs, and documented during onboarding. Transaction files and compliance documents stay inside your systems — DocuSign, Paperless Agent, your Google Workspace — organized to a naming convention we agree on before I touch a live file.
Do you already know my systems?
I work daily in TransactionDesk, CREA WebForms, OnePoint Matrix, Cornerstone Matrix, KW Command, DocuSign, Matterport and Google Workspace. If you run something different, expect a short ramp-up rather than a long one — the underlying workflow is the same.
What is your turnaround on offers and listings?
Offers and transaction paperwork are drafted the same business day they come in. Listings are prepared as the media and measurements arrive, with room dimensions pulled from the Matterport tour and details verified against MLS data before anything goes live.
How do you use AI tools in the work?
For drafting and speed, never for final judgment. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity help me produce first drafts of listing copy and social posts and research market context faster — every figure, date and condition is still checked against the source document before it reaches you or a client.
How do we start?
Email me at kaye.virtual@gmail.com with your current volume and what you want taken off your plate first. We set a short discovery call, agree scope in writing, and run a first week where I shadow your real files before working independently.
